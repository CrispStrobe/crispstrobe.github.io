Brickwright Privacy Policy

This policy covers CrispStrobe’s Brickwright workbench on the web and in its native apps.

Projects and settings

You can use Brickwright without a Brickwright account. Brickwright stores your local projects, settings, and lesson progress in your browser or on your device. You can remove local data through the relevant browser or operating system settings.

Features that use the network

  • Hosted compilation sends the source being compiled to the selected compiler service.
  • Remote extensions, toolchains, and machine images request the resources you choose. An extension may also communicate with its own services. The operator of each external service handles the information it receives under its own privacy practices.
  • When you import a project from a URL, your browser or app makes a request to the chosen host. Sharing and hardware features can connect to a service or device that you select.

As with other web requests, the destination may receive connection information such as your IP address. Hosting providers and third-party services have their own privacy practices.

Device permissions

Features can request access to Bluetooth, USB or serial devices, the local network, the camera, the microphone, speech recognition, or files you select. You can manage access in your browser or system settings.

Contact

For privacy questions, email cstr+apple@mailbox.org.
